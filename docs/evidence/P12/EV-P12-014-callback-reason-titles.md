## Third independent review (REQUEST_CHANGES at `ce3479e`: F2, F3, F4 open; F1 closed and preserved)

**Why the earlier grammar failed.** Each rule was a clause heuristic: a date counted when it stood anywhere in a clause that named the topic, a count was the number in front of "Personen" wherever it stood, and a refusal was a fixed list of phrasings. Proximity is not association: `bitte am Freitag telefonieren` shares a clause with `reservieren`, the `8` of `6–8 Personen` is a number in front of "Personen", and `nie`, `auf keinen Fall`, `nicht erwünscht` were simply not in the list.

**Design now (same module, same closed title grammar):**

- **Refusal first.** Negation words (`nicht`, `nie`, `niemals`, `keinesfalls`, `keineswegs`, `auf keinen Fall`, `unter keinen Umständen`) next to the callback word, with only a closed filler list between; `Rückruf … (nicht erwünscht | nie | auf keinen Fall | erübrigt sich | hat sich erledigt …)` ending its clause; `bitte nicht mehr anrufen` / `rufen Sie mich nicht an` ending the clause; `vergessen/unterlassen/stornieren Sie den Rückruf` (but not `vergessen Sie nicht den Rückruf`). A refusal wins over any positive wording, so contradictory instructions are unsupported. A positive request with unrelated negation stays a callback.
- **Counts.** A chain of numbers joined by `–`, `-`, `/`, `+`, `bis`, `oder`, `und`, `zu`, `plus` is captured as one mention (`6–8`, `sechs bis acht`, `zwischen 6 und 8`) and is not a number, so it cannot certify its endpoint; a hedge or bound directly before or after a count (`ungefähr`, `bis zu`, `mindestens`, `plus` …) makes it uncertain. Any uncertain mention anywhere voids the count. Exact only when every mention is exact, they agree, and one is stated "für/mit N" in a booking clause.
- **Dates.** Every date mention gets one role. It is the booking's only inside an explicit phrase with the topic: `<topic> [für N Personen] <für|am|auf|zum <Tag> | im <Monat>>` (not when a callback word sits within four words after it) or `<Datum> [Gäste] [einen|ein|Tisch …] <reservieren>`. Otherwise it is the callback's (a callback clause with no topic, or a callback word within four words) or unknown. One booking date and no unknown date that differs: stated; else omitted.

**Reproductions and results.**

- F2 A `Ich möchte reservieren und bitte am Freitag telefonieren. Rückruf bitte.` gave `Rückruf: Reservierung am Freitag`, now `Rückruf: Reservierung`.
- F2 B `Bitte wegen einer Reservierung am Freitag zurückrufen, dann bin ich erreichbar.` gave `… am Freitag`, now `Rückruf: Reservierung`.
- F3 `Bitte Rückruf. Reservierung für 8 Personen, es werden 6–8 Personen.` gave `… für 8 Personen`, now `Rückruf: Reservierung`.
- F4 `Bitte nie zurückrufen.`, `Bitte auf keinen Fall zurückrufen.`, `Rückruf ist nicht erwünscht.` were callbacks (and the first two reached `captureCallback` as HTTP 201); all three are now unsupported, HTTP 422.
- Controls kept: `Tisch für Samstag reservieren` → `am Samstag`; `Geburtstagsfeier im Dezember` → `im Dezember`; callback Friday and reservation Saturday (also in one sentence) → Saturday only; `Reservierung für 8 Personen` → 8; `8 Personen und 8 Personen` → 8; `Ich möchte nicht online reservieren. Bitte rufen Sie mich zurück.` stays a callback; the founder sentences and the F1 tests are unchanged.

Real-PostgreSQL: `expected 201 to be 422` for the three new refusals, from the mutation run. **GREEN:** 194 unit tests; 68 tests in the two work integration suites; the refusal test checks, per utterance, HTTP 422, the `unsupported-request` problem and the unchanged row count of every public table (tasks, callback acceptances, outcomes, audit events and the rest), and no table holds the utterance text.

## Fourth independent review (REQUEST_CHANGES at `1608108`: F2 and F3 again; F1 and F4 accepted)

**Counterexamples.** F2 `Ich möchte am Freitag die Reservierung telefonisch besprechen. Bitte Rückruf.` gave `… am Freitag`. F3 `Bitte Rückruf. Reservierung für 20 Personen, es werden fünfzehn bis 20 Personen.` gave `… für 20 Personen`; `… für 8 Personen, es werden null bis acht Personen.` gave 8.

**Root causes.** F2: the date-before-noun rule treated the noun "Reservierung" as evidence that a nearby date is the booking's. F3: the range detector only knew supported number words, so an unknown lower endpoint (`fünfzehn`, `null`) made the range invisible and its upper endpoint looked exact.

**Structural correction (same module, closed titles).**
- Dates. A date is the booking's only in two explicit constructions: `<topic noun> [für N Personen] <für|am|auf|zum Tag | im Monat>` where the noun is indefinite or bare (not after `die/der/dem/den/diese`, a possessive, a verb of discussing within three words, or a word of an existing booking such as `habe`, `gebucht`), no callback or contact word follows within four words, and, inside a callback clause, only the `für <Tag>` form; or `<date> … reservieren` (the verb, never the noun). A birthday celebration is exempt from the article rule only. Compound nouns (`Reservierungsänderung`, `-bestätigung`, `-stornierung`) make the title neutral. Any other date is the callback's or unknown and is omitted; one booking date and no differing unknown date, else none.
- Counts. A count expression is classified before any value is chosen: the word chain before `Personen/Gäste` is read whole, and any word before a range connector (`bis`, `oder`, `und`, `zu`, `–`, `-`, `/`, `+`) makes it a range, whether or not the word is a supported number. A hedge, a past or conditional marker, any further number in a clause that is not about the callback (`7 sind möglich`, `nein 5`; times with `Uhr` and ordinal dates are excepted), or an unsupported word voids the count. Exact only when all mentions agree and one is stated `für/mit N` in a booking clause.
