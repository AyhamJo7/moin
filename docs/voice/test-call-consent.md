# Einwilligung für Testanrufe (EXT-20)

- **Status:** DRAFT — engineering draft, not reviewed by counsel (EXT-02)
- **Purpose:** P04.05.02 — fifty scripted test calls need two volunteers besides the founder

> Drafted now so that it is not written in a hurry on the day of the calls. It covers a narrow
> case: a volunteer reads a prepared card into a telephone, and the call is recorded **on their own
> device**. No customer data and no real caller is involved at any point.

## What the volunteer is agreeing to

|                                    |                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **What happens**                   | You call a test number and read aloud from a card: a name, a telephone number, a postcode, a date and time, and a number of people. The assistant repeats back what it understood. You end by pressing a key. |
| **What is recorded**               | The call is recorded **on your own phone**, by you. Nothing is recorded by the system you are calling.                                                                                                        |
| **What the recording is used for** | Measuring how accurately German speech is transcribed and how long the assistant takes to reply.                                                                                                              |
| **What is stored on our side**     | The text the transcriber produced, the timings, and which card you read — in a file on a development machine. Not in the product, not in any cloud service.                                                   |
| **How long**                       | The recording and the text are deleted once the measurement is complete, unless you tick the second box below.                                                                                                |
| **Withdrawal**                     | You may withdraw at any time, without giving a reason, and everything from your calls is deleted. Write to _[privacy contact — pending DG-00]_.                                                               |
| **Voluntary**                      | Participation is voluntary. Nothing depends on it.                                                                                                                                                            |

## Notes for whoever runs the calls

- **Do not use a real customer, a real business number, or a real appointment.** Every value on the
  card is invented, and the cards are part of the test material.
- The second box is genuinely optional and genuinely separate. A single "I agree to everything"
  box is not consent to the second use.
- Collect the signature before the first call, not after.
- The consent form is filed outside this repository. This file is the text, not a record of who
  signed it.
