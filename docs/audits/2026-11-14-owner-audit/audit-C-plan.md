- **PG-3 (mixed):** every `[G:PILOT]` item VERIFIED — 214 sections in P00–P19, roughly 900 items, 0 tiers VERIFIED today; LG-V01…V14 (e.g. N ≥ 400 held-out intents per template, ≥ 150 real-network read-backs, ≥ 500 real-network turns for P95 < 1.8 s, 300 emergency cases); LG-P01/02/03/05/11/14 (restore drill, SEV1 fire drills, 7 green canary days, tabletops); real-network matrix over Gurlitt's forwarding; kill switch rehearsed; owner trained.

- **Engineering that cannot finish without a human:** anything measured on a real phone network or EU model (EXT-10/11/12), anything deployed (founder `apply`, EXT-09), disclosure wording (EXT-02/04), knowledge content (EXT-16).
